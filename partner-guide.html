How the day runs

  1. BeforeWe agree the class, the date, your rules, and what we will count on the day. You share one post or story with your members.
  2. Day beforeWe confirm the time and send you the check-in link with your studio’s name on it.
  3. 15 min before class endsWe set up one table near your door. Nothing for your team to do.
  4. Class endsMembers check in on their phones in 60 seconds, try a three-cup flight, and take home a free recovery guide.
  5. AfterWe clean up and leave the space as we found it.
  6. Next few daysYou get the numbers: who tried it, what they chose, who wants more. Then a short chat to decide together what comes next.

Questions partners ask

Which products do you use?

Products from a global nutrition company. Come to RECUP.STN HQ and we will show you the full menu and every label.

What does it cost our studio?

Nothing. Members pay a small price for the tasting flight, and we bring everything.

Will you sell to our members?

No. We pour, we listen, and we send members their free guide. If a member wants more, they ask us.

What if a member has a health condition?

The check-in asks the safety questions first. Anyone pregnant, under a doctor’s care, with kidney disease or on blood thinners is asked to see their doctor first.

What happens after the tasting?

We look at the numbers with you and decide together. Some studios want regular sessions. Some want the same picture for every client, every week. We can show you how when you are ready.
